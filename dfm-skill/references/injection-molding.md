# Injection Molding DFM

Use this reference for molded plastic, elastomer, or cast-like tooling decisions.

## Checks

- Wall thickness: prefer uniform walls and gradual transitions to reduce sink, voids, and warpage.
- Draft: add draft on faces parallel to pull direction; increase draft for textured surfaces.
- Parting line: identify a plausible pull direction, parting line, and shutoffs.
- Undercuts: flag side actions, lifters, collapsible cores, or redesign opportunities.
- Ribs and bosses: size ribs and bosses to avoid sink while preserving stiffness and fastening function.
- Gates and flow: consider gate location, weld lines, air traps, knit lines, and cosmetic surfaces.
- Ejection: ensure ejector access and avoid fragile features that stick in the mold.
- Corners: use radii to reduce stress concentration and improve flow.

## Common Actions

- Hollow thick blocks into uniform walls with ribs.
- Add draft and radii to all molded faces and transitions.
- Split complex parts or revise undercuts to avoid side actions.
- Replace deep solid bosses with cored bosses and ribs.
- Mark cosmetic surfaces and functional datums so tooling tradeoffs are explicit.
